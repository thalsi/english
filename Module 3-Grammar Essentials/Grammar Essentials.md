🟢 Parts of Speech (Most Important Grammar)

👉 English sentence = combination of these 8 parts
👉 If you understand this → your English becomes strong 💪

1. Noun (നാമം) – Name

👉 Person / Place / Thing / Animal

Examples:

Rahul (person)
Kochi (place)
Book (thing)
Dog (animal)

🟡 Sentence:

I have a book

2. Pronoun (സർവനാമം) – Replace noun

👉 Used instead of noun (repeat avoid)

Examples:

I, You, He, She, It, We, They

🟡 Sentence:

Rahul is my friend → He is my friend

3. Verb (ക്രിയ) – Action

👉 What someone does

Examples:

eat, run, write, speak

🟡 Sentence:

She runs
I eat food

4. Adjective (വിശേഷണം) – Describe noun

👉 Gives extra info about noun

Examples:

big, small, beautiful, fast

🟡 Sentence:

She is a beautiful girl
This is a big house

5. Adverb (ക്രിയാവിശേഷണം) – Describe verb

👉 Tells how / when / where

Examples:

quickly, slowly, now, here

🟡 Sentence:

He runs fast
She speaks slowly

. Preposition (സംബന്ധസൂചകപദം)

👉 Shows position / relation

Common words:

in, on, at, under, between

🟡 Sentence:

Book is on the table
Cat is under the chair

. Conjunction (ബന്ധകപദം)

👉 Joins words or sentences

Examples:

and, but, or, because

🟡 Sentence:

I like tea and coffee
I tried but failed

Interjection (ആശ്ചര്യം)

👉 Shows emotion 😲

Examples:

wow, oh, hey, alas

🟡 Sentence:

Wow! That’s amazing
Oh! I forgot

🔥 Easy Trick to Remember

👉 Sentence:
"She quickly eats a big apple in the kitchen and says wow!"

| Word    | Part of Speech |
| ------- | -------------- |
| She     | Pronoun        |
| quickly | Adverb         |
| eats    | Verb           |
| big     | Adjective      |
| apple   | Noun           |
| in      | Preposition    |
| and     | Conjunction    |
| wow     | Interjection   |
